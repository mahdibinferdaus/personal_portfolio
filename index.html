Mentoring for competitions

1:1 guidance for robotics competitions, science fairs, and innovation challenges — starting with a free intro call.

This is for students preparing for robotics competitions, science fairs, or innovation challenges who want a second pair of eyes on their approach. We start with a free 30-minute introductory call — not a sales pitch, just a real conversation about what you're working on and whether ongoing mentoring makes sense.

  • A free 30-minute introductory call
  • A read on where you're stuck and what to focus on next
  • An honest plan for what, if anything, makes sense going forward
  • Follow-up by email if something comes up before your next competition
What happens on the intro call?

We spend 30 minutes talking through what you're working on, where you're stuck, and whether ongoing mentoring makes sense for your goals. No pressure either way.

What competitions do you mentor for?

Robotics competitions, science and innovation fairs, and similar STEM challenges — things like WRO, Conrad Challenge-style programs, and school or national science fairs.

Is there a cost?

The introductory call is free. If we continue beyond that, pricing is worked out together based on what you actually need — there's no fixed rate.

How soon will I hear back?

Within 48 hours. If you have a competition date coming up, mention it in the form and I'll try to prioritize.
