Questions

A few things worth knowing.

Is the introductory call free?

Yes. The first 30-minute introductory conversation is free.

Is ongoing mentoring paid?

Yes. Continued mentorship after the introductory call is paid, with the scope and terms discussed based on your team's needs.

Is financial aid available?

Yes. Financial aid is available for eligible teams. Mention your situation in the request so it can be considered.

Do I have to commit to mentoring after the intro call?

No. The introductory conversation is also a chance for both sides to decide whether working together makes sense.

What if I need help with something other than Conrad Challenge?
